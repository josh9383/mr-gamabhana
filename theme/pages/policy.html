Gamabhan - Content, AI Use & Editorial Policy

For meaningful, experience-rich education

Gamabhan is currently an independent, non-profit educational initiative in its beta/pilot stage. It develops and curates supplementary educational content intended to help make learning more meaningful and experience-rich through observation, curiosity, experiments and experience.

Gamabhan is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official initiative of any school, education board, government institution, university, non-profit organisation or other institution unless explicitly stated.

Human and AI Contributions

AI is used as a supporting tool in the creation and curation of Gamabhan content. Human judgement remains central to the educational direction, validation and publication of content.

Human contributions

The following activities originate from or remain under human responsibility:

  • Ideation - the original teaching idea or activity originates from a human contributor.
  • Educational context - humans identify the concept, learner context and intended educational purpose.
  • Validation - humans review whether an idea is relevant, practical and appropriate for the intended learners.
  • Citations and sources - humans identify, verify and provide relevant sources and references.
  • Editorial review - content is reviewed before publication.
  • Final publication decisions - AI-generated or AI-assisted output is not intended to be published without appropriate human review.

AI-assisted activities

AI may be used for:

  • Speech-to-text - converting a contributor's spoken idea into text.
  • Structuring ideas - converting free-form ideas into structured components such as concepts, activities, questions and props.
  • Concept and prop mapping - suggesting relevant concepts and available materials.
  • Curation assistance - providing preliminary assessments of complexity, affordability, availability of props, preparation requirements, setup time and classroom feasibility.
  • Structured content generation - generating or assisting with JSON, Markdown and other publishing formats.
  • Image generation - creating illustrative or supportive images for activities and concepts.
  • Language and editorial assistance - improving wording, organisation and presentation.
  • Quality and duplication checks - identifying potentially similar ideas, inconsistencies or missing information.

AI assessments and suggestions are not treated as final educational judgements. They are inputs to a human review process.

Identification of AI Use

Gamabhan aims to clearly indicate when AI has made a significant contribution to text, images, structured data or the curation process.

AI-assisted content should not be presented as entirely human-created when AI has made a material contribution.

Educational Responsibility and Limitations

AI-generated output can contain factual, linguistic, contextual or educational errors. Human review is therefore an important part of the Gamabhan content process.

Gamabhan content is intended as supplementary educational material. It is not a replacement for textbooks, prescribed curricula, teacher judgement, professional teacher development, or the educational processes of a school.

Teachers and other users should consider their learners, classroom context, available resources and local requirements before using an activity.

Observation, Curiosity and Experience

Gamabhan's purpose is not simply to create more educational content.

It aims to support learning in which students can:

  • observe,
  • ask questions,
  • explore,
  • experiment,
  • experience,
  • and discover concepts through meaningful activity.

Gamabhan therefore seeks to develop and curate activities that are, where appropriate:

  • observation-based,
  • curiosity-driven,
  • experiment-oriented,
  • experience-rich,
  • based on readily available materials,
  • and practical to conduct in real classrooms.

Gamabhan is intended to support teachers, parents, peers and other participants in education, not to replace their role.

Current Stage - Beta / Pilot

Gamabhan is currently in an experimental beta/pilot stage.

The initial pilot focuses on Grade 4 Mathematics in Marathi-medium primary education. Content, curation methods, user experience and AI-assisted creation processes are being developed and improved through practical use and feedback.

The current content should therefore not be considered complete, comprehensive or final.

Feedback, real-world use and suggestions for improvement are an important part of the project's development.

Non-Profit and Institutional Independence

Gamabhan is currently being developed as an independent, non-profit initiative.

Its purpose is to make meaningful, experience-rich educational opportunities more accessible to students rather than to generate commercial profit from educational content or services.

Gamabhan is not an official initiative of any government department, local authority, school board, educational institution or non-profit organisation.

Any future institutional partnership or affiliation will be explicitly identified.

Sources, Citations and Intellectual Integrity

Where external sources, references, textbooks, educational materials or ideas from other creators are used, Gamabhan aims to provide appropriate attribution and references.

AI-generated suggestions are not automatically considered original or verified educational sources.

Sources and permissions are reviewed as far as reasonably possible before publication. Where the rights to use third-party material are unclear, Gamabhan aims to obtain appropriate permission or use an alternative source.

Student Privacy and Safety

Gamabhan aims to minimise the collection of personally identifiable student information.

Student names, photographs, contact information or other personal information should not be unnecessarily included in Gamabhan content.

If future AI or digital features involve student-related data, privacy, safety, human oversight and the best interests of children will be given priority.

Corrections and Feedback

Gamabhan is a continuously evolving project.

If an activity contains:

  • a factual error,
  • an educational concern,
  • an incorrect citation,
  • an inappropriate image,
  • an impractical activity,
  • or another issue,

users are encouraged to report it.

Content may be corrected, revised or removed based on feedback and subsequent review.
